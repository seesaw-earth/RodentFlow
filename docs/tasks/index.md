# Tasks

RodentFlow 2.1 analyses **25 behavioural tests from video with pose keypoints**, plus **multi-chamber tail suspension** and **pose only**, which exports the keypoints and kinematics for your own analysis. Each page below explains the design of the test, why keypoints measure it better than a body centroid, exactly how each measure is defined, a simulated example, and the papers behind it.

Every task runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The task decides how many animals are tracked; for one-animal tasks you choose a dark or white coat, for two-animal tasks two dark mice or one dark and one white (see [Models](../models.md#coat)).

## Results without video {#manual}

Marble burying, sucrose preference, beam walking, gait, two-armed bandit and your own measures can also be entered by hand or imported from a table, on iPhone and iPad. These results take part in group comparisons like any other. See [Manual entry & importing tables](../guide/manual-and-import.md).

::: tip About the example figures
The example on each page is a **simulated session**: a model mouse is moved through the arena and the measures are computed with simplified versions of the app’s definitions. The figures show what each measure means; they are not data and are not meant as reference values.
:::
