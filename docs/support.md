## Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub: **[github.com/seesaw-earth/RodentFlow/issues](https://github.com/seesaw-earth/RodentFlow/issues)**

To help us help you, please include:

1. **App version**: shown under iPhone Settings › General › iPhone Storage › RodentFlow.
2. **iPhone model and iOS version.**
3. **Assay and source**: video, manual entry or import.
4. **What you did, what you expected, and what happened.**
5. If it concerns tracking: a **screenshot of the preview** and, if you can share it, a short clip.
6. If it concerns an import: the **first lines of the file**, with real animal IDs replaced if needed.

::: warning Mind what you share
GitHub issues are public. Do not post unpublished data, identifiable information, or videos you are not allowed to share. A screenshot or a few anonymized rows are usually enough.
:::

## Feature requests

New assays, metrics or export formats are welcome as GitHub issues. Please describe the protocol, the measure you need, and how it is defined in your field. A reference to a methods paper helps.
