# Assay protocols & related tools

## Assay protocols

- Open field — [Seibenhener & Wooten 2015, JoVE](https://doi.org/10.3791/52434)
- Elevated plus maze — [Walf & Frye 2007, Nat Protoc](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2007.44)
- Light–dark box — [Bourin & Hascoët 2003, Eur J Pharmacol](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0014-2999(03)01274-3)
- Novel object recognition — [Leger et al. 2013, Nat Protoc](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2013.155)
- Three-chamber social approach — [Yang, Silverman & Crawley 2011, Curr Protoc Neurosci](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21732314/)
- Social interaction after social defeat — [Golden et al. 2011, Nat Protoc](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2011.361)
- Y-maze — [Kraeuter, Guest & Sarnyai 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4939-8994-2_10)
- Vicarious trial and error — [Redish 2016, Nat Rev Neurosci](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2015.30)
- Morris water maze — [Vorhees & Williams 2006, Nat Protoc](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2006.116)
- Barnes maze — [Pitts 2018, Bio-protocol](https://bio-protocol.org/e2744)
- Fear conditioning — [Curzon, Rustay & Browman 2009 (NCBI Bookshelf)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK5223/)
- Looming escape — [Yilmaz & Meister 2013, Curr Biol](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24120636/)
- Tail suspension — [Steru et al. 1985](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00428203); [Can et al. 2012, JoVE](https://doi.org/10.3791/3769)
- Cylinder test — [Schallert et al. 2000, Neuropharmacology](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0028-3908(00)00005-8)
- Gait from pose — [Sheppard et al. 2022, Cell Rep](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2021.110231)

## Design, reporting and tools

- [ARRIVE 2.0 guidelines](https://arriveguidelines.org/) for reporting animal research
- [“What exactly is ‘N’…?”](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.2005282) — Lazic et al. 2018, PLOS Biol
- [BORIS](https://www.boris.unito.it/) — free manual behaviour scoring for validation
- [DeepLabCut](https://deeplabcut.github.io/DeepLabCut/), [SimBA](https://github.com/sgoldenlab/simba), [keypoint-MoSeq](https://keypoint-moseq.readthedocs.io/) — downstream analysis of exported keypoints
- [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr/) — free video converter
